    What is simulated vs functional

    • Simulated: the Alexa+ conversational surface and external execution of reminders/messages.
    • Functional: sample selection, multi-step state, editable response drafts, contextual outputs, guided demo and audit trail. All interpretation and output content is predefined; no LLM or real Alexa service is called.
    • Production path: document parser/OCR, authenticated calendar/email tools, consent gates and encrypted retention.

    Important: LifeDesk organises and summarises information. It is not legal, financial, medical or professional advice and does not guarantee that an interpretation is correct.
